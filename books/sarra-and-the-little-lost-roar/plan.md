# Sarra and the Little Lost Roar

A small voice. A big friendship.

Original read-aloud animal adventure for Sarra, approximately ages 4–7. English and French.

## Story and visual direction

Sarra does not solve the problem by making the cub louder. She listens, stays beside him, and lets his voice return when a friend needs encouragement. The refrain invites the child to listen, rather than delivering a lesson.

Cream cold-press paper, loose fineliner, transparent sepia/sienna/indigo washes, sage and honey accents. Sarra wears a mustard pinafore, cream shirt and teal boots; the honey-gold cub has a small russet mane and teal scarf. Neither is based on a supplied portrait.

Gelasio book serif for story lines, Fredoka for expressive sounds; use the app’s existing text compositor on quiet paper. No text embedded in spreads.

## Production

The images, manifests, original model and installer live together in this folder. Keep the book as a reviewable draft until the family chooses to share it. Do not seed accounts or reset any database.

Fallback import: drop cover.png, all ten spread images and pages.json into the admin studio with two-page spreads enabled. Set Sketchbook 3:4, configure the seven experiences from experience.json, and compile. The generated installer handles this for the local app.
