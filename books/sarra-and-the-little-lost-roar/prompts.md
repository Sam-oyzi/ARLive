# Illustration prompts — Sarra and the Little Lost Roar

Built-in image tool; cover + ten spreads = 21 pages. Generated assets are saved beside this file. The prompts below reproduce the final art direction, including continuity corrections. Use the cover and spread-01 as references.

## Cover · save as cover.png

```
The front cover of the same hand-painted sketchbook: a single portrait page of thick cream cold-press watercolour paper, photographed from straight above, 2:3, 1024×1536. One watercolour vignette in loose black fineliner ink with sepia, raw sienna and indigo washes, the same style as the spreads. The paper fills the whole image edge to edge: no desk, no frame, no border.
Sarra has warm medium-brown skin, a dark brown curly bob, mustard pinafore over a cream long-sleeved shirt and teal boots. The honey-gold lion cub has a small round russet mane, teal neck scarf and kind dark eyes. Keep their reference proportions, clothes and colors. A small rabbit stays below a standing child’s knee; the tortoise shell stays below the cub’s chest; elephant calf is larger than the child at equal depth. Use a coherent medium-wide view and explain size differences only through perspective.
Sarra crouches and listens to the shy cub beside a forest stream; owl, rabbit, elephant calf and tortoise around them. Large elegant black hand-lettered serif title: "Sarra and the Little Lost Roar". No other words.
```
